繁體中文

本政策說明「工地快拍板」（以下稱「本 App」）如何處理您的資訊。本 App 的設計原則是資料留在您的裝置上：本 App 不需要註冊帳號，也沒有開發者自己的伺服器，不會把您的照片或專案資料上傳到開發者。

1. 我們不蒐集的資料

  • 不要求姓名、電話、電子郵件等個人資料，也不需要登入。
  • 不包含廣告 SDK、第三方數據分析或追蹤工具。
  • 開發者無法存取您拍攝的照片、小白板內容、專案名稱或報告書。

2. 儲存在您裝置上的資料

您拍攝的照片與影片、浮水印與小白板文字、專案資料、缺失追蹤紀錄和匯出的 PDF／Excel 檔案，都只存在本 App 位於您裝置上的儲存空間。解除安裝本 App 或清除 App 資料後，這些資料都會被刪除（您已另外分享或匯出到其他位置的檔案除外）。

4. 地址查詢（反向地理編碼）

開啟 GPS 浮水印時，本 App 會使用 Android 系統內建的地理編碼服務，把經緯度轉換成地址。系統服務可能會把座標傳送給裝置的服務提供者（通常是 Google）處理。查詢得到的地址會印在照片浮水印上。這項服務受 Google 隱私權政策規範。若您不想使用這項服務，請關閉 GPS 浮水印或拒絕位置權限。

拍照時取得的經緯度與地址會連同該張照片的紀錄，只儲存在本 App 於您裝置上的私有儲存空間，並會出現在您自行產生及分享的 PDF 報告書與 Excel 照片清單中。這些資料不會傳送給開發者。本 App 查詢 Google Play 購買狀態與地址時所需的網路連線，是由 Android 系統服務及 Google Play 處理；天氣與環境監測資料的連線說明見第 5、6 節。

5. 天氣資訊（Open-Meteo）

天氣浮水印為選用功能，預設關閉。開啟後，拍照時本 App 會把當下的經緯度傳送給 Open-Meteo 免費天氣服務，取得氣溫、濕度、風速與天氣狀況，並印在照片上。傳送的內容只有座標，不包含照片或其他資料，也不需要帳號或 API 金鑰。Open-Meteo 的資料處理適用其使用條款與隱私權政策。關閉天氣浮水印後就不會再傳送。

6. 環境監測 API（由您自行設定）

您可以自行輸入工地環境監測系統的網址（例如 PM2.5、噪音、風速感測器），讓本 App 在拍照時讀取數值並印在照片上。本 App 只會向您設定的網址發出讀取要求；您輸入的網址、標頭與 API 金鑰只儲存在您的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者。這些資料的處理適用該監測系統提供者的政策。

7. 付款與訂閱

VIP 訂閱與終身方案一律透過 Google Play 帳款服務處理。付款資訊由 Google 蒐集及處理，開發者無法取得您的信用卡或付款資料。本 App 只會在裝置上向 Google Play 查詢購買狀態，用來開啟 VIP 功能。您可以隨時到 Google Play >「付款和訂閱」>「訂閱」管理或取消訂閱。相關處理適用 Google 隱私權政策。

8. 分享與匯出

只有在您主動按下「分享」或「匯出」時，檔案才會交給您選擇的其他 App（例如通訊軟體、雲端硬碟或電子郵件）。之後的處理適用該 App 的隱私權政策。

9. 兒童隱私

本 App 以工程從業人員為對象，並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。

10. 資料安全

資料存放在 Android 為每個 App 提供的私有儲存空間。建議您為裝置設定螢幕鎖定，並定期備份重要的匯出檔案。

11. 政策變更

若本政策有變更，我們會更新本頁內容與生效日期。重大變更也會在 App 內或商店頁面上通知。

12. 聯絡我們

如有任何隱私權相關問題，請來信：pyappsA@outlook.com

English

This policy explains how SiteBoard Cam (the "App") handles your information. The App is built so that your data stays on your device. There is no account and no developer-operated server, and your photos and project data are never uploaded to the developer.

1. What we do not collect

  • No name, phone number, email address or login is required.
  • No advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or tracking tools are included.
  • The developer has no access to your photos, blackboard text, project names or reports.

2. Data stored on your device

Photos and videos, watermark and blackboard text, project data, defect records and exported PDF/Excel files are kept only in the App's storage on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them, except for files you have already shared or exported elsewhere.

4. Address lookup (reverse geocoding)

When the GPS watermark is on, the App uses the Android system geocoding service to turn coordinates into an address. That system service may send the coordinates to the device's service provider (usually Google) for processing. The resulting address is printed on the photo watermark. This processing is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. To avoid it, turn off the GPS watermark or deny the location permission.

The coordinates and address captured with a photo are stored with that photo's record only in the App's private storage on your device. They also appear in PDF reports and Excel photo lists that you create and share yourself. They are never sent to the developer. The network access needed for address lookup and for checking Google Play purchases is handled by Android system services and Google Play. Weather and environmental-monitoring requests are described in sections 5 and 6.

5. Weather (Open-Meteo)

The weather watermark is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, the App sends the current coordinates to the free Open-Meteo weather service when you take a photo, and prints the temperature, humidity, wind speed and conditions on the photo. Only the coordinates are sent: no photos or other data, and no account or API key. Open-Meteo's terms and privacy policy apply to that request. Nothing is sent once the weather watermark is off.

6. Environmental monitoring API (set up by you)

You can enter the web address of your site's environmental monitoring system (for example PM2.5, noise or wind sensors) so the App reads the values when you take a photo and prints them on it. The App only sends requests to the addresses you enter. The addresses, headers and API keys you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. The monitoring system provider's policies apply to that data.

7. Payments and subscriptions

VIP subscriptions and the lifetime plan are processed only through Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment details, and the developer never receives your card or payment information. The App checks your purchase status with Google Play on your device only to unlock VIP features. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

8. Sharing and export

Files leave the App only when you tap Share or Export and choose another app, such as a messenger, cloud drive or email. That app's privacy policy then applies.

9. Children

The App is meant for construction professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

Data is kept in the private app storage that Android provides for each app. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping backups of important exported files.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the App or on its store listing.
